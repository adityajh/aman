## Core Product Rules

1. **Universal Measurement**: All product options include ORS & SRS sliders, check-ins, and automated clinical deterioration flags.
2. **Active Client Fence & Exemption**: Standard Deepen tiers cap open client records at 30 active clients (`clients.isActive = true`). Deepen Pro (`planTier = "pro"`) and Admin Exemptions (`isExempt = true` / `FREEBIE`) have **unlimited active clients**.
3. **Terminated Records**: Terminated clients do not count toward the active client capacity and remain fully accessible for historical reporting.
