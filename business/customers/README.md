# customers/

Empty until the beta starts.

## What goes here

- **Beta interview notes.** One file per person, `YYYY-MM-DD-firstname.md`.
- **The support log.** Every question asked, with the answer given. With three beta users, the support log is the research.
- **Objections.** What made someone hesitate or say no, in their words.
- **Testimonials**, with written permission recorded in the same file.

## Rules

- **Never a real client's name or details.** These files are about counsellors, not the people they see. If a counsellor mentions a client, anonymise before it is written down.
- Record what was said, not what it means. Interpretation goes in a separate note.
- Quote verbatim where you can. Paraphrase flattens the useful parts.
- One question is worth more than the rest: **what did you spend money on in the last year to become a better counsellor, and roughly how much?** The pricing thesis rests on the answer.
