The name · August 2026
fathom.
/ˈfaðəm/
Old English fæðm. The length of a man's outstretched arms. Also: arms, grasp, embrace.
Etymonline
A unit of measurement that began as a pair of open arms. Nine hundred years later we are building software that measures how a person is doing, and tries to be kind about it.

One word, three jobs

Each meaning arrived separately. All three describe the product.
Old English
To hold

Six feet. As wide as a person can reach. The unit is a human body, not a machine's idea of distance.

The measure stays human sized.
c. 1600
To sound

To take soundings. Drop a weighted line and find out how much water is under you, while there is still time to turn.

A score each session. The flag.
1620s
To understand

To get to the bottom of something. "I can't fathom it." The only word in English that means both measuring and understanding.

Numbers turned into meaning.

The story we tell

Short, true, and it sticks.
Before sonar, one sailor stood at the bow with a rope.

A lead weight on a knotted line. He threw it forward, let the boat come over it, and called out how deep the water was. Again. And again. All night if he had to.

He was not grading the river. He was telling the pilot what was underneath them, early enough to steer.

"By the mark, twain."Two fathoms. Twelve feet. Safe water. Samuel Clemens liked the call so much he took it as his name.

That is the job. Someone watching the depth so the person steering can do their work. A counsellor sitting with a client cannot also be watching the numbers. fathom holds the line.

Taking a sounding
The brand's depth ramp is the same idea. Light at the surface, dark as it goes down.
Why this matters commercially. A name people can retell is a name that travels. This one comes with a story a counsellor can repeat at a supervision group in thirty seconds, and it ends on the product's actual promise.

What it costs us

Stated plainly, because the case is weaker if we hide it.
The crowded word problem

Four software companies already use Fathom. Fathom.video is an AI meeting notetaker in daily use across professional life. There is also Fathom Analytics, FathomHQ in financial reporting, and a Fathom in health AI.

What that means. We will never own this word globally. If we take the outcomes wedge international in years three to five, our buyer already has a different Fathom open in another tab.

Why we take it anyway. None of the four is in India. None is in therapy. Four Fathoms coexisting is itself the evidence that a fifth in a separate category is defensible. And our first three years are India only, where the word is clean.

The mitigation. Never ship the word alone. Every title, listing and bio reads fathom, outcome tracking for counsellors. Growth here comes from supervision circles and referrals, not from winning a search result.
