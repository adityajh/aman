The combo: deep teal (the water you fathom — trust meeting growth) · terracotta (fired clay — the human warmth, used only for human moments, never for data verdicts) · unbleached paper and ink (the instrument). Chart series order teal → violet → terracotta → blue passes all colour-vision checks in both modes; the first three are safe in any chart form. Green is retired from the status palette — next to a teal brand it can’t be told apart — so “ahead” is quiet ink with a teal check, and only “look closer” gets a colour. For a product whose users internalise verdicts, that restraint is the brand.
