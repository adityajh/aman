## The decision

**Switched to Deepen. deepen.health as primary, deepen.co.in for India. Both bought on the day of the sweep. deepencare.com left open for now.**

Reasoning:

1. Deepen keeps most of the fathom story. Depth, going below the surface, understanding. The two words share a root idea. The lead-line story survives almost intact.
2. "Deepen your practice" is already the phrase counselling CPD uses. The mission line is *empowering mental health practitioners*. Deepen states it.
3. deepen.health is a better asset than anything fathom can get without paying an aftermarket seller.
4. Cleaner trademark field. One notable holder, Deepen AI, in autonomous-vehicle data. Category distant.

**If Fathom stays:** take fathomcounsel.com and fathomcounsel.in together. Matched pair, both free, and "counsel" names the buyer. Accept that the clean word is gone for good.

**Do not** buy an aftermarket fathom domain. Total founder runway to break-even is ₹2.5 to 3 lakh. A parked .com would eat a meaningful share of it before a single user pays.
